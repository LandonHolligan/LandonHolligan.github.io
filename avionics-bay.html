Sounding Rocket Avionics Bay

Avionics Structures Lead · CU Sounding Rocket Laboratory

As Avionics Structures Lead, I am leading a six-person team through the structural design and analysis of the avionics bay for SRL’s space-shot rocket. I designed the overall bay structure and used modal and random-vibration analysis to identify a resonance risk in the board sleds, leading us to add stiffeners that raised their natural frequencies and prevented excessive motion between the plates.

Designing the bay

I designed the overall avionics bay structure and the board sleds that hold the electronics inside the nosecone. My work covers the mechanical layout and structural design, with CAD reviews to keep the individual parts working together as one assembly.

Analysis that changed the design

I ran modal and random-vibration analysis on the sleds to understand their response to the rocket environment. The results identified a resonance risk and drove us to add stiffeners to the plates, raising their natural frequencies to avoid coupling with rocket motor excitation.

Leading the team

I lead six members, assign tasks, run meetings, and review CAD. I have approved multiple designs and spend time helping new members learn design and modeling techniques, including how vibration and shock affect the avionics structure.
