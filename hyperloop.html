Hyperloop TBM Soil Gripper

Soil-gripper design and structural calculations · CU Hyperloop

I designed the soil-gripper subsystem for CU Hyperloop’s tunnel-boring machine, including the protective shroud and structural load path. I performed most of the tear-out and weld calculations for the gripper connections.

Gripping the tunnel wall

I developed the soil-gripper concept for the tunnel-boring machine. Inflatable tubes press against the tunnel walls to react the digging loads, while the surrounding structure transfers those loads back into the machine.

Shroud and load path

I designed the protective shroud around the grippers to address punctures, bulging, and force transfer. I worked through how the load travels from the grippers into the supporting structure and connections.

Structural calculations

I performed most of the tear-out and weld calculations for the gripper connections. Those checks helped size the attachment geometry and evaluate the structural load path. This was my main analysis contribution alongside the gripper design.
